# Inter-Agency Jurisdictional Matrix: DPH Environmental Health (HHVC) vs. Department of Building Inspection (DBI)

**Document ID:** HHVC-POLICY-2026-08-16-JUR-01  
**Category:** `hhvc-policy`  
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-16  
**Authoritative Authorities:**
- San Francisco Health Code Article 11 (Public Health Nuisances & Vector Control)
- San Francisco Health Code Chapter 41 (Residential Hotel Sanitation)
- San Francisco Housing Code Chapters 1–14
- San Francisco Building Code & Planning Code
- California Health & Safety Code § 17920.3

---

## 1. Overview & Statutory Scope

The City and County of San Francisco divides housing habitability and safety enforcement between two primary regulatory agencies:
1. **Department of Public Health (DPH) — Environmental Health Branch / Healthy Housing & Vector Control (HHVC):** Enforces biological health hazards, disease vectors, unsanitary environmental conditions, rodent/insect infestations, animal nuisances, and public health nuisance abatement under **SF Health Code Article 11**.
2. **Department of Building Inspection (DBI) — Housing Inspection Services (HIS) & Code Enforcement:** Enforces physical, structural, electrical, mechanical, heating, plumbing, and building maintenance standards under the **SF Housing Code** and **SF Building Code**.

To ensure clear guidance for residents and avoid routing complaints to the "wrong door", city information must strictly differentiate which department holds primary inspection and enforcement jurisdiction.

## 3. Boundary & Overlap Rules

### Mold & Moisture: DPH vs. DBI
- **DPH HHVC Jurisdiction:** Visible surface mold, mildew resulting from humidity, lack of bathroom ventilation, or tenant sanitation conditions under Health Code Art. 11 Sec. 581(b)(6).
- **DBI HIS Jurisdiction:** Water penetration caused by building defects, including leaking roofs, defective gutters, burst interior plumbing pipes, or foundation seepage under SF Housing Code Sec. 701.
- **Cross-Referral Standard:** When an HHVC inspector discovers that visible mold is caused by an active plumbing or roof leak, HHVC issues a sanitary citation while simultaneously submitting a formal cross-agency referral to DBI Housing Inspection Services.

### Residential Hotel (SRO) Inspections: DPH vs. DBI
- **DPH HHVC:** Conducts annual routine health inspections of SRO residential hotels for communal kitchen hygiene, shared toilet/shower sanitation, garbage chutes, and vermin control under Health Code Chapter 41.
- **DBI HIS:** Conducts habitability inspections for room dimensions, structural partitions, fire separations, heating units, and unpermitted electrical hotplates.

## 4. Resident Referral & 311 Triage Standard

To ensure high-fidelity citizen self-service across all digital touchpoints:
1. **Primary Entry Point:** Residents should be directed to **San Francisco 311** (`sf311.org` or phone 311), which acts as the centralized intake dispatch engine.
2. **Online Touchpoints:** Digital service transactions must provide explicit "Need another City service?" links directing structural/heat complaints to DBI and public water line issues to SFPUC.
3. **No Retaliation Rule:** Both DPH and DBI enforce the fundamental civic rule that reporting housing conditions to municipal inspectors is a legally protected right (SF Rent Ordinance § 37.9(a)(8), CA Civil Code § 1942.5).
