# Get help with vermin in your building

Learn how to deal with rodents, bedbugs, mosquitos, or pigeons are in your home or business.

## What to know

### Number to use

- Calling within San Francisco: 311
- Calling from outside the City: 415-701-2311

### Search health violations

- [Residential violations](https://xnet.sfdph.org:8443/ords/eeop/f?p=119%3A1)
- [Hotel violations](https://xnet.sfdph.org:8443/ords/eeop/f?p=120%3A1)

## What to do

### 1. Understand your responsibilities

Building owners and tenants have responsibilities to keep vermin away. [Learn more about keeping your building free of pests](https://www.sf.gov/information/keeping-your-building-free-vermin).

### 2. Report a vermin issue

Call 311 to report health issues connected to businesses, apartments, or other living and working spaces.

[Report an issue](https://www.sf.gov/report-health-nuisance-or-hazards)
