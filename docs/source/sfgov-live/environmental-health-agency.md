# Environmental Health

We help San Franciscans live and work safely by inspecting food facilities, housing, and commercial environments.

## Highlights
- [Food Safety Inspection Scores](https://www.sf.gov/food-safety-inspection-scores): Check restaurant sanitation scores.
- [Permit Center Services](https://www.sf.gov/permit-center): Apply for environmental health permits and renewals.
- [Report a Public Health Concern](https://www.sf.gov/report-a-health-nuisance-or-hazards): File a complaint about pests, mold, food safety, or housing conditions.

## Spotlight

### Come to the Permit Center
Visit our service counter at 49 South Van Ness to speak with an inspector, submit plan reviews, or pay annual municipal permit fees.
[Learn more about Permit Center hours and services](https://www.sf.gov/permit-center)

## Resources

### Programs and services by topic
- [Healthy Housing and Vector Control](https://www.sf.gov/topics--healthy-housing-conditions): Housing inspections, bed bugs, rodents, and moisture guidelines.
- [Food Safety and Inspections](https://www.sf.gov/food-safety): Restaurant permits, retail food safety, and mobile food facilities.
- [Water Quality and Wells](https://www.sf.gov/water-quality): Recycled water, swimming pools, and private wells.
- [Hazardous Materials and Waste](https://www.sf.gov/hazardous-materials): Chemical storage compliance and cleanup oversight.

## About
The Environmental Health Branch protects and promotes public health across San Francisco through community education, regulatory enforcement, and routine compliance inspections.
