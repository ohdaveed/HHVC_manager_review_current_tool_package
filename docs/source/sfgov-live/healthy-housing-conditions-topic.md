# Healthy housing conditions

We help keep San Francisco housing healthy by protecting residents from vermin and lead poisoning.

## Services

### General housing issues
- [Report a residential building concern](https://www.sf.gov/report-residential-building-concern): Report habitability, maintenance, or safety concerns in residential housing.
- [Report low water pressure or loss of water service](https://www.sf.gov/report-low-water-pressure-or-loss-water-service): Contact the SFPUC to report water service disruptions.
- [Get help with vermin in your building](https://www.sf.gov/get-help-vermin-your-building): Learn how tenants and landlords can resolve infestations.
- [Pay your annual healthy housing fee](https://www.sf.gov/pay-your-annual-healthy-housing-fee): Property owners can pay required municipal environmental health fees online.
- [Report a noise problem](https://www.sf.gov/report-noise-problem): Guidance on reporting excessive neighborhood or commercial noise.
- [Report unsafe construction work involving asbestos](https://www.sf.gov/report-unsafe-construction-work-involving-asbestos): Report demolition or remodeling without proper containment.

### Lead poisoning issues
- [Stop construction work spreading lead paint dust](https://www.sf.gov/stop-construction-work-spreading-lead-paint-dust): Report uncontained exterior or interior scraping/sanding.
- [Prevent lead poisoning in young children](https://www.sf.gov/prevent-lead-poisoning-young-children): Learn risk factors, screening recommendations, and prevention measures.
- [Report damaged paint](https://www.sf.gov/report-damaged-paint): Report peeling or chipping lead paint in pre-1978 residential buildings.
- [Invite your landlord to fix lead dangers](https://www.sf.gov/invite-your-landlord-fix-lead-dangers): Connect property owners with grant programs.
- [Apply to fix leaded paint and soil](https://www.sf.gov/apply-fix-leaded-paint-and-soil): Low-income property owners may qualify for lead remediation grants.

### More services
- [Get lead training and certification](https://www.sf.gov/get-lead-training-and-certification): State and local courses for contractors and renovators.
- [Sign up to do lead-related inspection or construction](https://www.sf.gov/sign-do-lead-related-inspection-or-construction): Registration requirements for certified workers.
